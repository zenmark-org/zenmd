## Added

- Image zoom: click an image to see it large; zoom, pan and fit. It works in exported sites too.
- A calmer home screen with the new logo.
- The Inspector, a side panel with the page's file, its content and its issues.
- The Developer tab, with the app's log and the plugins' errors.
- Signed and notarised releases: the DMG opens without a warning.
- The sidebar menu: Open, Open in Editor, New Page Here…, Move Up and Down, Copy Path, Copy Link, Reveal in Finder and Hide from Sidebar.
- Reorder the sidebar by dragging, or with ⌥↑ and ⌥↓; the order is saved as `nav: order`.
- File › Close Folder (⇧⌘W).

## Changed

- The sidebar's Open… button is gone; open a folder from the home screen or with ⌘O.
- The new Zenmark logo.

## Fixed

- The logo on the home screen shows in the app.
