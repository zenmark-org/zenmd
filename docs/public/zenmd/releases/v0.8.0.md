## Added

- Lini figures next to mermaid: flows, mindmaps, sequences, charts, floor plans and circuits from a `lini` block, on by default.
- Diagrams from files: `{file=diagrams/flow.mmd}`, a path after the language, or a `:file:` line. A path starting with `/` is from the folder's root.
- Zoom and pan inside diagrams, as on GitHub, and Expand to see one over the whole window.
- Wide tables open over the window with Expand, with the header row in view.
- The sidebar's Pages | Files switch shows the folder as it is on disk; `zenmd tree` prints either tree.
- The rest of MyST: callouts, figures and tables, math, tabs, dropdowns and cards, roles, cross-references, includes and glossaries.
- Pages are checked against their page template and layout: Problems › Structure, and `zenmd check`.
- zenmd for Linux, a GitHub Action that builds a docs folder into a website, and opening a single markdown file on its own.
- A Finder icon for markdown files.

## Changed

- The sidebar tree starts collapsed, and the reader is wider (94 characters by default).
- The settings folder is `zenmark`, not `zen`; an old one is moved once.
- Page templates are labelled Built-in, Edited or Custom.

## Fixed

- Big diagrams no longer freeze the window, and diagrams no longer flash their source while they load.
- The Inspector counts the build's page templates, and Problems in the editor lists the page's structure problems.

The full list is in the repository's CHANGELOG.md.
